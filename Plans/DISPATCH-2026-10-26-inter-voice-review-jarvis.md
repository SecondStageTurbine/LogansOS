## Checklist

Answer each item PASS or FAIL with `file:line`. For a FAIL, give the file, the bytes or the test, and the wrong result.

1. **The font is upstream and unmodified.** `assets/fonts/Inter-Regular.ttf` (411,640 bytes) has the sha256 the README
   names (`40d692fc…`), and that matches `extras/ttf/Inter-Regular.ttf` in `rsms/inter` v4.1's `Inter-4.1.zip`
   (archive sha256 `9883fdd4…`). Fetch the archive with `curl` or `git`, not through a summarizing tool, per the
   byte-oracle rule. `shipped_fonts_are_canonical_sfnts` passes for it.
2. **Licence.** `assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt` is the OFL-1.1 text with Inter's copyright line. xtask stages it to
   `/licenses/OFL-Inter.txt` (`LICENSE_FILES`). `NOTICE` names both OFL files. The Plex OFL stays because Plex Mono
   still ships.
3. **Only the proportional voice changed.** The `plex-sans` feature became `inter` everywhere it was used (neo-text,
   neo-arthur, neo-atrium, neo-worker, arthur-logic-tests). No binary still links Plex Sans, and nothing else switched
   face: Plex Mono and the kernel console font are untouched. `IBMPlexSans-Regular.ttf` was removed from `assets/`;
   nothing still references it.
4. **The loosened oracle bound.** The exact-area oracle's mean per-pixel bound went from 0.35 to 0.4
   (`userland/neo-text/src/tests/mod.rs`). The reason given is that Inter's `a` at 13 px puts 23 of its 63 pixels on
   curved edges, for a mean of 0.365. The max-2 bound is unchanged. Recompute that mean yourself and say whether 0.4
   is the tightest sensible bound or hides a real rasterizer error. One check: does any case besides `a` at 13 px now
   pass only because of the looser bound?
5. **Goldens re-baselined, none dropped.** Each re-baselined golden (the `fixtures/` scene and label digests, the
   neo-text `exact_*`, `ft_*` and `hb_kern_sans` tables) was regenerated by its oracle script, not hand-edited, and
   every test that existed before #145 still exists and runs. Count them before and after.
6. **Kerning.** `scripts/fonts/harfbuzz_kern_oracle.py` regenerates `hb_kern_sans.rs` from Inter with HarfBuzz, and the
   in-tree shaper matches it for the tested pairs.
7. **Footprint.** The image grows by roughly the font-size difference (411,640 vs 200,500 bytes). Nothing else balloons
   (neo-arthur's and neo-atrium's binary sizes before and after, if you build them).
8. **Text and docs.** The Absent-model line reads exactly "I can hear you, but my model isn't on this system yet. Boot
   the LogansOS USB it came with." everywhere it's asserted. Design language §6.1 names Inter.
