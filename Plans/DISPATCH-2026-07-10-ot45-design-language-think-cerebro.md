## The one idea (frame everything around it)
**Beauty here is not decoration bolted onto plumbing — it is the capability model made visible.** The Atrium's signature is that **a surface's MATERIAL carries its trust state** (owned / tethered / untrusted / denied), so *security becomes the beauty* — the thing every other OS hides in nagging dialogs, we render as the aesthetic itself. The grammar must be **grammar, not uniformity** (Article 1.6): one consistent system of spacing/light/material/motion/type/color that *preserves distinct per-thing identity* — distinct silhouettes are recognition, and recognition is the five-year-old test applied visually. The cautionary tale to design against (prior-art trap): the macOS Tahoe/Liquid-Glass episode — Apple forced every icon into one rounded-rect and made a once-beautiful system bland AND harder to use. Enforced sameness is the documented failure mode; refuse it.
