## 0. The authoritative identity (standardize on this)
**NeoPlutarch/LogansOS is a single-node, capability-secured, AI-native _monolithic_ kernel in Rust** — internal discipline via **Rust module/crate boundaries + I-14 fault-containment**, NOT address-space service isolation. Reconciliations (from ADR-001, keep them straight):
- **"Monolithic" ⊥ "capability-secured"** — orthogonal. Capabilities govern *userspace* authority (I-15, the cap-check, Art 29.3); monolithic is about *internal* kernel structure. We are both.
- **Our fault-containment is Rust + I-14, not isolation.** A microkernel buys containment via address-space walls; we buy it via Rust memory-safety + I-14 + lock discipline. (And it's a sharper story after the HarmonyOS research: the strongest shipping *microkernel* OS's demonstrated weakness is memory-corruption — the exact class we close by construction; we chose monolithic *because* Rust lets us have the performance without that exposure.)
- **HongMeng/HarmonyOS IS a microkernel** ("Microkernel Goes General"); seL4 is a microkernel. Those references are correct and stay.
