   tracked heaps, uses <=16MiB chunks, and returns null when add or mapping
   fails. The 64-region cap can strand a newly mapped range until exit, but
   cannot hand it out through an untracked heap or extend across a stack.
   A highly aligned/large request may fail conservatively. No out-of-map
   allocation path was found in the changed code.
