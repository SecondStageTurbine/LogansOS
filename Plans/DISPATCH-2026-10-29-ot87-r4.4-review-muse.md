3. Both syscalls return `EPERM` without the cap and `ENOSYS` without `installer`.
4. `SYS_INSTALL_LIST` writes only within `[buf, buf+len)` after `validate_user_buf`, and only whole records.
5. `SYS_INSTALL_RUN` installs only to an enumeration the last list returned. It re-runs the full preflight (R4.1's
   claim, with its identity and fingerprint checks), and can't reach a refused row's disk.
6. LIST takes and releases each claim on every path, and nothing writes (`installer_ui::list`, `install_claim::claim`).
