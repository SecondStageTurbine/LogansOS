3. **PASS — dropping the waiting frame's Arc under the waiters lock is sound for published, waitable processes.** At `process.rs:3307`, no completion is recorded and the waiters guard is still held. Either must acquire this same lock before releasing that reference. The registry supplies the reference before teardown begins. Thus this decrement cannot run the target's final destructor while the waiter holds the lock, and the resumed path reads the current thread's blocked result rather than the target (`scheduler.rs:1948`). `unregister_unstarted` (`process.rs:2134`) is an explicit exception to “every teardown completes waiters,” but the failed-enqueue path (`:3563`) has not returned a PID to its spawner or published a user ProcessHandle; its unstarted child cannot have acquired an ordinary SYS_WAIT waiter. Spawn's local/thread anchors also exist during that failure cleanup. CAP_GRAPH's deferred `Process` ownership (`:2437`, `:2455`) adds retention and does not bypass completion for a published target. If the premise were broken, final Process field drops could release handles/Pmap under waiters; `pmap.rs:1195` performs the synchronous shootdown. The proof must stay scoped to published waitable targets, not arbitrary test-only unregisters by PID.
