The caller's strong SeqCst CAS and lazy call are in one `without_interrupts` closure (`kernel/src/test_harness.rs:29348-29358`; wrapper `kernel/src/arch/x86_64/mod.rs:516-520`). If the holder swaps first, CAS sees RELEASED and loses, including the interval before the actual unlock. If CAS wins first, the holder has not swapped and therefore has not unlocked. The holder is enqueued on the caller's CPU (`kernel/src/sched/kthread.rs:272-275`; `kernel/src/sched/scheduler.rs:697-728`), and the winning caller does no yield between CAS and call: only an atomic tick read. Thus the same-CPU holder cannot run in the old preemption gap. When the VFS body later enables IF, the holder can resume; that enablement is the behavior under test. Each attempt joins its holder before the next reset (`test_harness.rs:29359`), preventing an old holder from supplying a later attempt's HELD state.
