## Your facet — the compat layer, the compartment, the impedance, the substrate
1. **The bounded "gaming compartment" (Option C — the leading candidate; design it deeply).** Can a **confined, kill-switched, cap-contained Linux-compatible subsystem** host the real Linux gaming stack (Mesa+Proton+Steam) at **near-native** speed (GPU/input/audio *brokered*, not VM-emulated) *without* ambient authority leaking to the core? Design the **isolation seam**: what the compartment gets (a brokered GPU-submit cap, audio-out cap, controller-in cap, save-file cap, net cap) and what it structurally *cannot* touch (the cap-native core, other principals, arbitrary files). Is it GPU-passthrough-to-a-contained-Linux-personality? A cap-brokered Proton? A new primitive? Compare against a VM (why near-native-container beats VM) and against SteamOS's immutable-base model.
2. **The Win32/Proton-on-non-POSIX question.** How deeply is Proton/Wine welded to POSIX (threads→pthreads, fds, the loader, signals)? Is the honest path "run *real* Proton inside the Linux-personality compartment" (Option B/C) rather than "reimplement Win32 on libneo" (Option A)? Apply your std-coupling rigor: quantify the POSIX-substrate an unmodified Proton needs.
3. **The capability-vs-ambient + anti-cheat impedance — the novel-tech angle.** Design the **cap-brokered game bundle** (framing §6.1): a game as a confined bundle whose only powers are brokered device caps — gaming as the *showcase* of isolation, not a breach. And the honest anti-cheat verdict (kernel-AC irreconcilable → out-of-scope-by-design; is there any cap-native integrity story a studio could ever trust?).
4. **Steam-the-app + DRM.** What hosting the Steam client (or GOG Galaxy) actually needs; Steam DRM vs GOG DRM-free; downloads/updates/the runtime — inside the compartment.
5. **The latency substrate (M5).** The gaps to close for playable games: **audio** (OT-46 is open — what a low-latency PipeWire-class output needs), **controller/gamepad** (HID + XInput/rumble on our USB stack — extends the input work), **frame-pacing/scheduling** (game-loop scheduling, big allocations), and where our compositor/USB-HID work already helps.
