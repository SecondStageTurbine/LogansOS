- **ISC-1 — Kernel-wide clippy paydown (the recurring OT-tracked debt).** `cargo clippy` on the kernel currently fails `-D warnings` on pre-existing lint (the step is non-blocking/run-and-report today). Pure lint fixes — **no behavior change**; if any lint can't be fixed without behavior risk, `#[allow]` it *with a one-line justification* rather than changing logic. **REQUIRED: first REMOVE the crate-root `#![allow(clippy::…)]` batch in `kernel/src/main.rs` added by Arthur Slice 3 (commit `2066ee4`) to pass the workspace clippy gate** — it *masks* the debt (`collapsible_if`, `needless_return`, `too_many_arguments`, `manual_range_contains`, `manual_contains`, `doc_lazy_continuation`) rather than fixing it. Remove the blanket allows and **actually fix** each lint (or keep a *specific* `#[allow]` at the offending item with a one-line justification — never a blanket crate-root allow). **KEEP** the `#[cfg(not(clippy))]` panic-handler gate in `kernel/src/panic.rs` (legitimate — it lets `clippy --workspace` build the no_std kernel without colliding with std's `panic_impl`; not a behavior change). - **ISC-2 — Workspace-hygiene guard (root-cause the #243 class).** Add a structural guard so a `std`-requiring crate can **never** unify into the no_std userland binaries again (the `crypto_common`→`panic_impl` trap): the right mechanism (resolver-2 feature isolation / a per-target build boundary / a CI assertion that the no_std userland binaries build clean in the full-workspace context).
