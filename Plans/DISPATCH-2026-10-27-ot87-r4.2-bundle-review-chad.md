## Checklist

Answer each item PASS or FAIL with `file:line`. For any FAIL, give a concrete input.

1. **The `NEOINST1` oracles are independent (I-12).** The bundle and slot-map-entry fixtures are hand-laid, not
   produced by the writer, and the parser rejects each malformed field: bad magic, an overflowing length, a manifest
   hash mismatch, a truncated part.
2. **The ESP templates.**
   - Each is valid FAT32 for its sector size.
   - Limine's `BOOTX64.EFI` is the pinned version.
   - The slot files are preallocated and contiguous.
   - The read-back that builds the slot map is our own code, not fatfs's, so a fatfs bug can't pass its own check.
   - A non-contiguous chain fails the build (KAT).
3. **Slot sizes.** 72 + 4×44 MiB per slot, two slots, fit the 512 MiB ESP with FAT overhead. They cover §7.2's
   measured kernel and module sizes with margin. Say what happens when a future kernel outgrows 72 MiB: the build should
   fail, not truncate.
4. **The pin.** `install-bundle` is reproducible: build twice, get identical bytes. `--check` refuses a wrong pin.
   Nothing in the bundle depends on the time, the path or the host.
5. **Vendoring.**
   - `third_party/fatfs` keeps its MIT license and copyright.
   - `VENDORED.md` records the upstream URL and commit.
   - It's used only by xtask, not by the kernel or userland.
   - Its version and commit match THINK §11.2.
6. **Prior art.** The PR body restates THINK §11.2's rows with licenses and last commit dates.
