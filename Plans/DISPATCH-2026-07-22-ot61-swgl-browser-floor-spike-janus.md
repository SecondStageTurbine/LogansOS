## Why software-GL-first (the leverage)
Servo/WebRender paint against a GL device/context. Our hardware GL adapter (`surfman-neo`/`libneogl`) is the OT-63 graphics half — **not yet built**. `swgl` is Servo's own **software** implementation of that exact contract. If swgl compiles + runs on our host-closure surface, we get the *real* WebRender paint path with **zero hardware dependency** — unblocking the modern-site correctness battery now, with the hardware adapter dropping in behind the unchanged seam later (the Rung-0→Rung-1 graft). This decouples "does the browser render correctly?" from "is the GPU driver done?" — a large de-risk.
