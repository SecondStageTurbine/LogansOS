**What it does.**
- std's `sys::random::fill_bytes` calls `SYS_GETRANDOM`, chunked to the kernel's 64 KiB ceiling.
- On a kernel without `kernel-rng`, it aborts with 134 and has no weaker fallback.
- **`std_entropy`** (new, `-cpu max`) checks a RandomState HashMap, 8 distinct keyed states, and a 200 KiB fill.
- **`std_real_smoke`** also spawns the entropy ELF on a kernel without `kernel-rng`, and it must exit 134.
- **xtask** stages only the ELFs each std scenario spawns, which keeps it under the 8-module ceiling.
