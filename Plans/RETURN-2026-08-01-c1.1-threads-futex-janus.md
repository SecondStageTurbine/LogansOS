## Diagnosis: the reported exit 35 is not the scenario exit

I read all four serial dumps in workflow-dispatch run
[`30684105976`](https://github.com/SecondStageTurbine/NeoPlutarch/actions/runs/30684105976),
job `QEMU Integration Tests (shard 2/4)`. Its `head_sha` is
`c2e6cdda934e93cdf8d2b5e067fa846784a585e5`, exactly the pre-rebase source
that was inspected.

| Attempt | QEMU launcher status | `neo-init` serial exit | Last C1.1 line |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 35 | 39 | `FAIL: negative control passed (race did not reproduce)` |
| 2 | 35 | 39 | same |
| 3 | 35 | 39 | same |
| 4 | 35 | 39 | same |

All four attempts reached `wake-count: preserved … ok`; check 7's join arms
did not fire. The launcher status `35` therefore is not evidence that any
`neo-init` `return 35` executed. The serial dumps are conclusive, consistent,
and in the job log; no diagnostic artifact was added.

The actual C1.1 failure was a test-contract defect in check 7b. It treated an
already-enqueued waiter (the weak pre-syscall barrier happened to be safe on
that schedule) as a product failure. The corrected control reports a race only
when it observes `qlen == 0` and both finite wakes pick zero waiters. An
already-enqueued schedule is clean but inconclusive; the positive check 7
queue-length barrier remains the enforcement point.

The subsequent AAG condition correctly narrowed that repair: accepting an
inconclusive result let the negative control pass without exercising its
subject. The final control holds its waiters after their pre-syscall readiness
publication, requires `qlen == 0` and two zero-pick wakes, then releases them
through an EAGAIN value mismatch. It is deterministic and bounded; any other
outcome fails the scenario.

## Changes

- Tagged every pre-existing `return 35` site with an unambiguous serial site
  name, including the SLAAC and Atrium paths that share the numeric code.
- Replaced check 7's `filter_map` with explicit `None` rejection, and require
  exactly three successful joins before printing the wake-count success line.
- Kept serial diagnostics in the job log path only; no artifact behavior was
  introduced.
- Removed a duplicated `dw2-scene-policy` feature entry exposed by the rebase.
- Repaired the rebase-introduced DW1/DW2 capability-assembly split-brain:
  both scenarios appeared in the inner GPU-cap push but were omitted from the
  three outer cfg predicates that select the non-empty inherited-capability
  builder. Both therefore received the empty slice and exited 40.
- Closed the CP-36 lifetime invariant: `spawn_user` anchors its initial
  `Thread` to the `Arc<Process>` returned by registration, matching child
  threads before either can enter the scheduler. This protects
  `thread_join_for_current`'s raw registry pointer across its park/resume
  window.
- Made check 7 reject a missing spawn before its queue barrier and retain the
  exact successful-join count after all handles have been considered; no
  `filter_map` or coupled shared error flag can silently turn fewer threads
  into a three-thread pass.
- Tagged the check-7b and check-8 writer-join exit-40 paths, and gated
  `libneo::nthread::spawn_no_bootstrap` behind `libneo/neo-test`.

**OT-75 failure class:** an outer cfg excludes an authority the inner cfg would
push; compile success does not prove the scenario received its registered
authority. The structural guard is to keep every scenario authority in all
builder-selection predicates as well as its inner push predicate.
